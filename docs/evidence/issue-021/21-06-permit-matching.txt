== the key actually used for permits, and why it is reduced ==
The City of Rock Island permit reports publish a street line only — no city, no state, no ZIP.
The permit-side key is therefore street|rock island|il with an EMPTY ZIP5 component, matched
against parcels whose Site_City is ROCK ISLAND. This is a real reduction in key strength and
it is stated rather than hidden: a ZIP5 component would have made the key stronger and this
source does not carry one.
